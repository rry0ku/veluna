Veluna
Features

Crafted for Pure Listening

Everything you need to enjoy, discover, and own your music library without commercial interference.

Ad-Free Music Streaming
Search and stream any song or music video instantly from YouTube Music. Zero advertisements, unrestricted high-fidelity audio, and pure extraction via a native Rust backend.
High-Speed Downloader
Save any track or playlist directly to your drive in high-bitrate MP3, Opus, or M4A format with bundled ffmpeg. Complete with automatically embedded ID3 tags and high-resolution album art.
Synced and Romanized Lyrics
Real-time scrolling lyrics synchronized to playback with an immersive full-screen Theater Mode. Includes automated Romanization transliteration for Japanese, Korean, and Chinese tracks.
Listen Together
Host or join real-time synchronized listening sessions with friends. Share queues and enjoy the exact same music in perfect lockstep from anywhere.
In-App Track Comments
Read and explore community discussions directly inside the music player as you listen. Connect with how fellow listeners feel about each track.
Tempo and Pitch Controls
Fine-tune your audio with precision tempo adjustment, real-time pitch shifting, variable playback speeds from 0.5x to 2.0x, sleep timers, and gapless transitions.
Playlist Import and Export
Effortlessly migrate your library: import Spotify playlists via Exportify CSV or paste any public YouTube playlist URL for automated track matching.
Listening Stats and Scrobbling
Gain deep insights into your listening habits with interactive charts and time-of-day breakdowns. Integrated dual scrobbling for Last.fm and ListenBrainz, plus Discord Rich Presence.
Zero Telemetry and Privacy
No tracking scripts, no analytics, no cookies, and zero telemetry. Your playlists, history, and listening stats live strictly on your own device in an encrypted local database.
